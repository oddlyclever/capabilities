## Across the modules

What spans several modules, or the repository as a whole, is filed by kind.

- [Architecture Decision Records](Decisions): one hard-to-reverse choice each, and why the alternatives lost.
- [Product Requirements](Requirements): what a capability must do, and for whom.
- [Research](Research): investigations and what they found.
- [Specifications](Specifications): how a thing is, maintained in place.
- [Developer Guides](Guides): how to build, test and release the libraries.
- [Reference](Reference): external facts kept close, such as formats, standards and upstream behaviour.
